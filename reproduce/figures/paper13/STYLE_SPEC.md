# Paper 13 final figure style specification

## Output
- PDF for final manuscript use.
- SVG as editable/vector companion.
- PNG at 600 dpi for review.
- White background.
- No font files committed or redistributed.

## Typography
- Arial when lawfully installed locally.
- Large uppercase panel letters.
- Sentence-case titles and axes.
- Readable manuscript-scale labels.
- Explanatory prose belongs in captions when it clutters artwork.

## Layout
- No clipping, legend/data overlap, title/panel-letter collision, or text touching card borders.
- Generous internal card padding with compact overall whitespace.
- Single-panel SI figures do not receive unnecessary panel letters.

## Color
- Restrained publication palette with consistent semantics.
- Include legend/color bar where categorical mapping is not self-evident.
- Figure 4B uses a discrete 0–3 risk-severity color bar.

## Scientific display rules
- `not scanned` must stay distinct from observed zero.
- Never manufacture uncertainty.
- S7 is not duplicate leakage.
- Figure 5D is rank-percentile agreement, not calibration.
- S8 selection is display-only.
- S9 is conceptual decision/reporting logic.
